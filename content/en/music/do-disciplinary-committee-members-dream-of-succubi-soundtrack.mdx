## Trailer Audio Is Not a Track List

The Steam page includes official trailers, which are the closest thing to an authorised preview of the game's audio outside the game itself. A trailer can use an edited cue, a shortened loop, or a mix created for marketing.

That means hearing a piece in a trailer does not prove its in-game title. It also does not prove that the full cue is available separately. Treat trailer audio as a preview of tone, not as a catalogue.

## How Music Fits the Game

The official description divides play between daytime city activities and nighttime exploration of Ligo and the Dream Corridor. It also includes seminars, jobs, patrols, puzzles, and Chosen battles.

That variety naturally calls for different musical moods. The store pages do not identify the instruments, themes, or composers, so an article should stop at the structural observation. Saying that the game needs day, night, and battle music is reasonable; naming a track called "Ligo Nights" is not.

The DLsite listing's 130 adult scenes and additional standing-art scenes are content counts, not music counts. Do not convert them into a soundtrack length.
