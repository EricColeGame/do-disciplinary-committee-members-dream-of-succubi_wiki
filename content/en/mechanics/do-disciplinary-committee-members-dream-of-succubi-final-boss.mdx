The **Do Disciplinary Committee Members Dream of Succubi final boss** is deliberately withheld by the official pages. Kagura Games and Steam describe a battle royale against the Chosen, where every fight is to the death, but they do not name the last opponent or publish attack patterns. That makes the endgame a test of the systems you have been using all game rather than a boss you can study in advance.

There is also a source problem around this topic. The official copy does not list the endings, and older guides have invented details such as an easy mode, a virgin ending, or a named "Hall of Memories." This page sticks to the official loop, the developer's Ci-en reply, and a clearly labeled Japanese community guide.

## What Is Known About the Final Opponent

The official premise is a proxy war. Iroha makes a Pact with Lily, becomes part succubus, and must defeat the Chosen, other humans who made pacts with demons. The store page says every Chosen battle is to the death and asks what that toll does to Iroha and the world.

The identity of the final Chosen is not documented officially. Any name you see in a guide is player-written or a spoiler from the game itself. There is no official HP table, phase list, or attack pattern to verify.

## The Two Winning Outcomes

Milky Way's August 17, 2025 Ci-en reply describes two winning outcomes. One erases Iroha's memories of the supernatural episode; the other lets her retain them. The developer says neither dramatically changes the supporting cast's circumstances. In the retention branch, Iroha is implied to become the antagonist's toy instead of Nashi Okane.

The official pages do not call either branch true, normal, or canon. The developer explains the aftermath, not the exact flag conditions.

## The Reported Loss Ending

The Japanese community guide at [think-game](https://think-game.com/archives/53900213.html) lists three ending events. ED01 and ED02 follow a win and a choice; ED03 「永遠の悪夢」 follows a loss to the final boss. This is third-party documentation, so treat the trigger and title as community-reported.

| Community label | Reported trigger | Source status |
|---|---|---|
| ED01 「現代に生きるサキュバス」 | Win, then choose | Third-party |
| ED02 「帰ってきた日常」 | Win, then choose the other option | Third-party |
| ED03 「永遠の悪夢」 | Lose the final battle | Third-party |

That loss ending is different from the mid-story defeat routes described on DLsite. A normal Chosen loss moves the story into a defeat route and continues; the final-battle loss is listed as an ending event.

## What Not to Believe

Several claims circulate without support in the official pages or the community guide used here:

- That the game has a selectable Easy mode.
- That a virgin playthrough unlocks its own ending.
- That the final boss requires a specific seduction debuff or environmental trick.
- That defeat routes are always six or more scenes long.
- That the gallery is called the "Hall of Memories."

Some of those may come from player experimentation, but they should be labeled as such. They are not official facts, and presenting them as confirmed makes an endgame guide less reliable.

## How to See Both Win Branches and the Loss Branch

1. Save in a manual slot before the final battle.
2. Win and take one choice.
3. Reload and take the other choice.
4. Reload and lose if you want to verify ED03.
5. Finish the game once before expecting a complete recollection room. The developer confirmed on September 17, 2026 that it cannot be fully opened before clearing.

No official source documents a New Game+ system, so do not assume clear data opens a bonus route.

## FAQ

**Is the final boss named on any official page?**
No. The official pages treat the last Chosen as a spoiler and publish no boss name, HP, or attack list.

**What level should I be?**
There is no official level target. Use the documented loop: earn money, buy equipment, attend seminars, solve puzzles, and train in the Dream Corridor.

**Is there an Easy mode?**
No selectable difficulty mode is documented on the official pages.

**Does losing the final battle end the game?**
A community guide lists a loss ending, ED03. That is separate from the mid-story defeat routes.

**Is there a virgin ending?**
No. A virgin run is a self-imposed challenge, not a scripted finale.
