## What Counts as a Choice in This Game

Do Disciplinary Committee Members Dream of Succubi is not built around a visible dialogue wheel. The choices that matter are mostly structural: how Iroha spends daytime and nighttime, whether she wins or loses a Chosen fight, and which option she takes after the final victory.

That distinction matters because fan discussion sometimes turns ordinary activity selection into a hidden route system. The [official Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4233960/Do_Disciplinary_Committee_Members_Dream_of_Succubi/) describes activities and consequences, but it does not publish a dialogue-choice flowchart.

## The Daily Choice: Day or Night

The official loop gives each period a different job:

| Period | Activities named by the publisher | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Daytime | Explore the city, attend seminars, work jobs, patrol | Skills, money, Charm, story context |
| Nighttime | Travel to Ligo and enter the Dream Corridor | Combat practice and strength |
| Puzzle time | Solve puzzles | Additional stat growth |

The Japanese DLsite page divides the day into morning, evening, night, and dream time. That does not mean every activity is exclusive to one block; it means the game presents multiple scheduling opportunities. If an activity is unavailable, advance the story or try another time period.

The practical choice is not "which route am I on?" but "what is currently limiting me?" If equipment is weak, work. If combat is unfamiliar, train. If a stat-boosting puzzle is available, solve it. This follows the publisher's own explanation of how the systems connect.

## Charm and Character Direction

The official page says work, training, and various activities raise Charm and make Iroha more demonic. It also frames the ending question as Iroha deciding which side of herself is real.

That is a thematic direction, not a published ending formula. No official source reviewed here states that a specific Charm value, costume, or conversation selects a named ending. Charm is worth raising because the game identifies it as a progression stat, but a fan-made threshold should be treated as unverified.

## The Defeat Choice

The Japanese DLsite description explains that losing to another Pact-holder does not stop the game. The story enters a defeat route, and Iroha continues after the humiliation while looking for a way back to her former life.

This creates a real strategic decision:

- Win and continue on the main story path.
- Lose and explore a defeat branch before the narrative moves on.
- Save before the encounter so the choice can be reversed.

No official page documents an auto-lose command. If you want to see defeat content without fighting badly, use a save and deliberately lose rather than trusting an unverified menu claim.

## The Final Choice

The ending decision is concentrated at the end. A Japanese community guide reports:

| Reported event | Trigger |
|---|---|
| ED01 「現代に生きるサキュバス」 | Win the final battle, then choose one option |
| ED02 「帰ってきた日常」 | Win the final battle, then choose the other option |
| ED03 「永遠の悪夢」 | Lose the final battle |

The developer separately confirms two winning outcomes: memory erasure and memory retention. The developer does not publish a one-to-one mapping to ED01 and ED02, so do not assume the first menu option always means "normal" or "true."

This is the only ending split with meaningful documentation. The best way to handle it is to save before the final battle and record what each option produces.

## Choices That Do Not Have Official Consequences

The following are not documented as ending selectors:

- A virgin playthrough.
- A particular costume or equipment set.
- An easy mode.
- A named affinity score.
- A "Hall of Memories" route.
- New Game+ choices.

The recollection room is the real gallery system. The developer said it cannot be fully unlocked before clearing, which makes completion a post-game activity rather than a hidden route.

## A Low-Regret Choice Plan

1. Alternate daytime and nighttime activity so money, skills, and combat practice do not fall behind.
2. Raise Charm through the activities the game names rather than chasing an unsupported breakpoint.
3. Complete available puzzles for their confirmed stat boosts.
4. Save before a Chosen fight and decide whether the defeat branch interests you.
5. Save again before the final battle and test both winning choices.
6. Reload once to verify the reported loss ending.
7. Return to the recollection room after clearing.

That plan does not guarantee a secret outcome because no secret outcome is documented. It does guarantee that you can observe the branches the sources actually support.

## FAQ

**Do dialogue choices change the story?**
The documented major choice is after the final battle. No official source publishes a complete dialogue-choice list or confirms that every conversation changes an ending.

**Should I lose a Chosen fight on purpose?**
Only if you want the defeat route. Winning continues the main story, and the game does not require a loss to reach the finale.

**Does Charm decide the ending?**
Charm is a confirmed progression stat, but no official threshold or ending-selection rule is published.

**Can I see all three reported endings in one run?**
Not without reloading. The two win outcomes are alternate choices, and the third is reached by losing.
