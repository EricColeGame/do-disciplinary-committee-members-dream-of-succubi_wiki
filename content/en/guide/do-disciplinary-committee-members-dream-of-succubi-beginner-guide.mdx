## What the Official Beginner Information Actually Covers

The [official Steam page for Do Disciplinary Committee Members Dream of Succubi](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4233960/Do_Disciplinary_Committee_Members_Dream_of_Succubi/) gives a clear starting point. Iroha is a disciplinary committee member who meets the succubus Lily on patrol and accepts a Pact. To complete it, she must defeat other humans who made pacts with demons, called the Chosen. The story asks whether she will survive, suffer a tragic defeat, or decide which side of herself is real.

What the storefront does **not** provide is a chapter-by-chapter walkthrough, a recommended build, or a complete list of every choice. This beginner guide therefore sticks to systems the publisher describes and marks the few community-reported details separately.

The game is a Windows title developed by Milky Way and published in English by Kagura Games. It supports English, Japanese, and Simplified Chinese, and its listed features are single-player, Steam Cloud, and Family Sharing.

## Understand the Two Halves of a Day

The central structure is easy to understand even before you open the game. Daytime is for city exploration, seminars, jobs, and patrols. Night is when Iroha travels to the succubus town of Ligo and enters the Dream Corridor to fight monsters and improve her combat skills.

| Period | Official activities | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Day | Explore the city, attend seminars, work jobs, patrol | Learn skills, raise Charm, earn money |
| Night | Visit Ligo and traverse the Dream Corridor | Fight monsters and practise combat |
| Puzzles | Solve puzzles encountered through play | Boost stats further |
| Story | Meet the Chosen and follow the Pact plot | Advance toward the finale |

The two halves feed each other. The official description says daytime experience helps Iroha learn new skills, while money from work buys equipment for combat. That makes a balanced approach the safest first assumption. Do not treat daytime as filler merely because the night section contains battles.

The Japanese DLsite listing adds a four-part description of the day: morning, evening, night, and the dream world. That is useful context, but the English store's simpler day/night framing is enough for planning.

## A Sensible First Run

There is no official optimal route. A low-risk first run looks like this:

1. Explore the city and learn which activities are available.
2. Take jobs when you need equipment money.
3. Attend seminars and complete stat-boosting puzzles rather than skipping every optional system.
4. Use the Dream Corridor to become comfortable with combat before treating every Chosen encounter as a damage race.
5. Keep manual saves, especially before major story encounters.
6. Finish the game once before trying to complete the recollection room; the developer stated on September 17, 2026 that it cannot be fully opened beforehand.

That last point is more useful than a speculative stat checklist. A full gallery is a post-clear goal, not evidence that you missed a secret route early in the story.

## Charm, Money and Equipment

The official page names **Charm** as a stat raised through work, training, and other activities. It also says that becoming stronger makes Iroha "more demonic than ever." The store does not publish exact thresholds, caps, or a list of every stat source, so a beginner should not rely on invented breakpoints.

Money has a clearer purpose: the official description directly connects earnings from work to powerful new equipment. If a fight feels unnecessarily difficult, check whether you have been ignoring paid upgrades rather than assuming the game requires a specific hidden build.

Puzzles are the third confirmed progression channel. They are described as stat boosts, so they are worth doing when available. No official source reviewed here states that a particular puzzle is mandatory or that one stat is superior.

## What Happens When You Lose

The Japanese DLsite description explicitly says that losing to a Pact-holder does not produce a game over. Instead, the story moves into a defeat route in which Iroha endures humiliation and looks for a chance to recover her former life.

This means defeat is both a narrative consequence and additional content. It does not prove that losing is the intended first playthrough. The English store copy still frames survival as a goal, and the developer describes two winning outcomes in an August 17, 2025 Ci-en reply.

For a first run, save before important fights. Win to continue on the main route, or reload if you want to explore a defeat branch without committing the rest of the playthrough to it.

## Endings Without a Fake Checklist

No official page reviewed here publishes an ending count or names a true ending. The developer describes two win outcomes: one in which Iroha's memories are erased and one in which they remain. A Japanese community guide lists three ending events, with the third reached by losing the final battle.

That is the honest limit of current documentation. A beginner does not need an ending checklist before reaching the finale. Keep a save before the last battle, then use it to test the post-victory options and the reported loss branch.

## Official Versions and Requirements

The English release is Steam app 4233960 for Windows. Steam lists an October 8, 2026 release date; Kagura's product page uses October 9. The Japanese PC original is DLsite work RJ01241743, released May 4, 2025. DLsite also lists a separate Japanese iOS/Android edition, RJ01398608.

There is no verified official English APK, Google Play release, console port, or free full-game download. Kagura hosts a free v1.05 patch page, but its "Steam and GOG Patch" label is not proof that a GOG release exists. Steam is the confirmed English storefront.

Minimum requirements are modest: Windows 8/8.1/10/11, an Intel Core2 Duo or better, 4 GB RAM, a DirectX 9 or OpenGL 4.1 capable GPU, and 1 GB of storage. The store warns that menu or map loading may cause brief lag.

## FAQ

**Is there an official Do Disciplinary Committee Members Dream of Succubi beginner route?**
No. The publisher explains the loop and systems but does not publish a route order. The plan above is a conservative interpretation of those systems, not a hidden official build.

**Should I skip daytime activities?**
No. The official page links daytime seminars and work to skills and equipment money. The game presents both halves as parts of the same progression loop.

**Can I complete the recollection room before finishing?**
The developer said it cannot be fully unlocked before clearing. Leave full collection for after the credits.

**Where should I buy the English version?**
Steam app 4233960 is the confirmed English release. DLsite is the official Japanese storefront, including a separate Japanese mobile edition.
