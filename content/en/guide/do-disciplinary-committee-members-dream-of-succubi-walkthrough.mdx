## A Walkthrough Without Invented Steps

No official full walkthrough exists for Do Disciplinary Committee Members Dream of Succubi. The publisher's [Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4233960/Do_Disciplinary_Committee_Members_Dream_of_Succubi/) explains the premise and loop, but it does not list map-by-map objectives, puzzle answers, or boss weaknesses.

This walkthrough therefore describes the verified shape of the game and the decisions that matter. It avoids pretending that a fan-made sequence is an official route. The most detailed third-party source used here is the Japanese [think-game guide](https://think-game.com/archives/53900213.html), which describes a prologue, chapters 1 through 5, and a final chapter.

## Before You Start

The English release is Steam app 4233960 for Windows. It supports English, Japanese, and Simplified Chinese and includes single-player, Steam Cloud, and Family Sharing. The original Japanese PC release is DLsite RJ01241743; DLsite also lists a separate Japanese iOS/Android edition, RJ01398608.

There is no verified English APK, Google Play release, console port, or official free full download. Use the official storefront rather than a repack site.

The story begins with Iroha Himeno, a disciplinary committee member, meeting Lily the succubus on patrol. Iroha accepts a Pact: win the demons' proxy war as an agent for the succubi and her wish will be granted. Her targets are the Chosen, humans who made pacts with demons.

## Prologue: Learn the Loop

The opening establishes the two halves of the game:

- Daytime: explore the city, attend seminars, take jobs, and patrol.
- Nighttime: travel to Ligo and use the Dream Corridor to fight monsters.
- Puzzles: solve them to raise stats.
- Story: identify hidden Chosen and deal with the consequences of the Pact.

Do not rush through the opening looking for a hidden route choice. No official source says the prologue contains an ending flag. The useful preparation is learning how menus, combat, equipment, and the day/night cycle fit together.

## Chapters 1 to 5: Build Broadly

The community guide names five numbered chapters between the prologue and the final chapter. It does not provide an official route order for every optional activity, and neither does the publisher.

A practical chapter routine is:

1. Finish the current story objective first so new systems and locations unlock.
2. Use daytime jobs when equipment is behind.
3. Attend seminars and complete puzzles that are available.
4. Train in the Dream Corridor before a Chosen encounter.
5. Check the status screen for Charm and other visible growth.
6. Keep a manual save before any fight that looks like a major story beat.

The Japanese DLsite page says a day is divided into morning, evening, night, and dream time. If one activity is unavailable, advance the current story scene or try a different time block rather than assuming the game is broken.

## Chosen Fights and Defeat Routes

The official premise says fights against the Chosen are to the death. Mechanically, however, the Japanese store explains that losing to a Pact-holder does not produce a game over. The narrative enters a defeat route, and Iroha continues looking for a way to restore her former life.

That means a loss is not a dead save. It is alternate content with a long-term consequence. Save before the encounter if you want to decide whether to follow that branch now or reload and win.

The DLsite page also describes training against "sukemon" monsters as combat instruction. Use those encounters to learn the battle system before a story boss forces the issue.

## Final Chapter: Save Before the Battle

The ending split is concentrated at the end. The community guide reports:

| Event | Reported trigger |
|---|---|
| ED01 「現代に生きるサキュバス」 | Win the final battle, then choose one option |
| ED02 「帰ってきた日常」 | Win the final battle, then choose the other option |
| ED03 「永遠の悪夢」 | Lose the final battle |

The developer's August 17, 2025 Ci-en reply separately describes the two winning outcomes as memory erasure and memory retention. It does not map those descriptions to ED01 and ED02 or publish the exact selection logic.

Keep a save before the final battle. Win once, record the result of each post-victory option, then reload and lose if you want to verify the third reported event. This is the only ending plan supported by the available evidence.

## After the Credits

The recollection room is the in-game gallery. The developer confirmed on September 17, 2026 that it cannot be fully unlocked before clearing. Finish the game before treating missing entries as a walkthrough failure.

No official source documents New Game+, a "true ending" flag, an easy mode, or a virgin ending. If a walkthrough presents those as facts, it is going beyond the published record.

DLsite lists the game at roughly 25 hours, depending on how many optional scenes are collected. A completionist run can therefore be much longer than a straight story run.

## FAQ

**Is there an official Do Disciplinary Committee Members Dream of Succubi walkthrough?**
No. The publisher describes systems and premise, not a step-by-step route. The chapter outline here relies on a clearly labeled community guide.

**What happens if I lose to a Chosen?**
The story enters a defeat route rather than ending immediately. Save beforehand if you want to keep the choice open.

**When should I save for the endings?**
Immediately before the final battle. The two win outcomes require different choices after victory, and the third is reached by losing.
