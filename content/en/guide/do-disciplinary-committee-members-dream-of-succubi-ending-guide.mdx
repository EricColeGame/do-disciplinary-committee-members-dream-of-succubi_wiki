## What an Ending Guide Can Honestly Promise

There is no official ending list, ending count, or named true route for Do Disciplinary Committee Members Dream of Succubi. The [Steam store page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4233960/Do_Disciplinary_Committee_Members_Dream_of_Succubi/) asks whether Iroha will survive or suffer a tragic defeat, but it does not explain the finale.

Two sources provide the useful detail. A Milky Way Ci-en reply dated August 17, 2025 describes two winning outcomes: memory erasure and memory retention. A Japanese community guide at [think-game](https://think-game.com/archives/53900213.html) lists three ending events, including one reached by losing the final battle. The first is developer information; the second is third-party documentation and should be labeled as such.

## The Finale Split at a Glance

| Reported outcome | Trigger reported by the community guide | Supporting context |
|---|---|---|
| ED01 「現代に生きるサキュバス」 | Win the final battle, then choose one option | One of two post-victory branches |
| ED02 「帰ってきた日常」 | Win the final battle, then choose the other option | The other post-victory branch |
| ED03 「永遠の悪夢」 | Lose the final battle | Loss ending |

The developer confirms two win outcomes built around memory, but does not map them one-to-one to the guide's ED01 and ED02 labels. Do not claim that one is officially "normal" and the other "true."

The same community guide describes the story as a prologue, chapters 1 through 5, and a final chapter. That structure is more precise than the unsupported "six chapters" shorthand, but it remains community documentation.

## Memory Erasure and Memory Retention

The developer's reply says the surrounding characters' circumstances do not change dramatically in either winning outcome. The distinction is what Iroha remembers.

In the memory-erasure branch, the supernatural episode is effectively removed from ordinary life. The developer describes it as close to nothing happening before or after the story, with the emotional effect carried by atmosphere. In the memory-retention branch, Iroha keeps her experiences. The developer adds that she is likely to become the antagonist's toy instead of Nashi Okane.

That is a character-level consequence, not a promise of a completely different world state. The reply also does not provide flags, affection thresholds, or a list of dialogue choices that determine the branch.

## How to See the Branches

The practical method is save-based:

1. Keep a manual save before the final battle.
2. Win and record the result of the first post-victory option.
3. Reload and choose the other option.
4. Reload once more if you want to verify the community-reported loss ending.
5. Clear the game before expecting the recollection room to be complete.

The developer stated on September 17, 2026 that the recollection room cannot be fully unlocked before clearing. That makes a completed first run more useful than trying to force every gallery entry early.

No official source reviewed here documents a New Game+ system. A clear save, the recollection room, and a new playthrough are not the same thing.

## Do Defeat Routes Count as Endings?

Usually, no. The Japanese DLsite description says losing to a Pact-holder shifts the story into a defeat route rather than ending the game. Iroha continues after the loss and looks for a way back to her former life.

Those branches contain substantial alternate content, but the community guide treats only the final-battle loss as ED03. Mid-story defeats are routes, not automatically separate endings. This distinction prevents a common mistake: counting every loss scene as a unique ending.

## What Stats or Choices Decide the Ending?

No official source publishes a required Charm value, relationship threshold, or dialogue checklist for the finale. The Steam page says Charm rises through work, training, and other activities, but it does not say that a particular value selects an ending.

The only documented ending decision is the reported choice after winning the final battle. Plan around that concrete split instead of inventing hidden requirements. If a guide claims that a specific costume, virgin status, or single dialogue line unlocks a secret ending, ask for a first-party source.

## What Not to Assume

- There is no official "true ending" label.
- There is no official ending total.
- There is no verified "memories ending" or "Hall of Memories."
- There is no documented easy mode that changes the finale.
- There is no verified English mobile or console version with different endings.

The recollection room is the actual gallery term. It is not an ending.
