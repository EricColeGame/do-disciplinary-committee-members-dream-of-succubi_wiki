## Start With the Systems the Game Confirms

Good Do Disciplinary Committee Members Dream of Succubi tips begin with what the game actually tells you. The [Steam store page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4233960/Do_Disciplinary_Committee_Members_Dream_of_Succubi/) describes a daytime city phase and a nighttime phase in the succubus town of Ligo. Daytime activities include seminars, jobs, and patrols. At night, Iroha enters the Dream Corridor to fight monsters and improve her combat skills.

The page also confirms that daytime experience helps her learn skills, money from work buys equipment, and puzzles provide additional stat boosts. Those four links are enough to build a sensible first-run routine.

## Tip 1: Treat Day and Night as One Loop

The biggest avoidable mistake is treating daytime as a break from the "real" game. The official description connects both periods to the same power curve.

| Activity | What it supports | Why not skip it |
|---|---|---|
| Jobs | Equipment money | Combat upgrades need funding |
| Seminars | New skills | Daytime experience feeds progression |
| Patrols | Story and city activity | Keeps the main premise moving |
| Puzzles | Direct stat boosts | Extra growth outside battle |
| Dream Corridor | Combat practice | Prepares Iroha for Chosen fights |

The Japanese DLsite page divides the day further into morning, evening, night, and dream time. That does not contradict the English day/night summary. It simply gives a more granular view of when activities become available.

## Tip 2: Raise Charm Deliberately

The official page names Charm and says it rises through work, training, and various activities. It also frames the story around Iroha becoming more demonic and eventually deciding which side of herself is real.

What the page does not provide is a target number, a soft cap, or a list of every Charm source. Do not follow a fabricated "required Charm" table. Instead, treat Charm as one progression track among several and check the status menu after trying a new activity.

If an activity offers money, a skill, and Charm, it is likely efficient. If it offers none of the confirmed benefits, it may be optional flavor or a story scene rather than a mandatory grind.

## Tip 3: Use the Dream Corridor Before It Is Urgent

The Dream Corridor is the official nighttime training area. Its stated purpose is to fight monsters and polish combat skills, which makes it the natural place to learn the battle system before a major story encounter.

Do not wait for a Chosen fight to discover how skills, items, and equipment interact. Spend time in the corridor when the pressure is low. The game also includes training against monsters designed by the succubi, according to the Japanese store description, and those encounters are presented as combat instruction.

No official source lists exact enemy levels or a guaranteed farming spot. If a player guide recommends one, verify that it is describing the current version rather than a demo or an older build.

## Tip 4: Convert Work Into Equipment

Money has a direct, official use: buying powerful new equipment. That makes equipment the easiest answer to a difficulty spike that is not obviously about tactics.

Before grinding for levels, check the shop and your current gear. The game may be asking for a purchase you have postponed. The same principle applies to skills learned through daytime experience; a new ability can matter more than a few extra fights.

The Japanese DLsite page lists version 1.11 as the then-current PC build and notes several post-release fixes. That does not mean every old guide is wrong, but a tip tied to a specific bug should be checked against a recent build.

## Tip 5: Save Before Chosen Fights

Losing to a Pact-holder does not produce a standard game over. The Japanese DLsite description says the story enters a defeat route, where Iroha endures humiliation and searches for a way back to her former life.

That design is interesting, but it also means a loss can commit you to a long branch. Keep a manual save before a major Chosen encounter if you want the option to continue on the winning path or inspect the defeat content separately.

This is especially important at the final battle. The documented finale has two post-victory options and a reported loss ending. A save immediately before the fight lets you see all three without replaying the entire game.

## FAQ

**What is the best first-run strategy?**
Use both daytime and nighttime activities, keep equipment current, do available puzzles, and save before major fights. No official source names a single optimal build.

**Should I lose on purpose?**
Only if you want to see a defeat route. Losing is supported by the design, but it is not required to finish the main story.

**Can I max Charm early?**
No official cap or threshold is published. Raise it through the activities the game identifies and use the status screen to judge progress.

**Is there an official English mobile version?**
No. The English release is Windows-only on Steam. The DLsite mobile SKU is a separate Japanese edition.
