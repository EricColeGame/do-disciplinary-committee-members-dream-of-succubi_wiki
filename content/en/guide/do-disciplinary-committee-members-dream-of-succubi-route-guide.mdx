## What a Route Guide Should Cover

Do Disciplinary Committee Members Dream of Succubi does not have an official route chart. The publisher explains the game's day/night loop and battle-royale premise, while the ending structure is documented through a developer reply and a third-party community guide. A useful route guide therefore separates confirmed structure from player shorthand.

The confirmed English release is Steam app 4233960 for Windows. The original Japanese PC release is DLsite RJ01241743. Both lead to the same core story; platform differences are a storefront issue, not evidence of separate route maps.

## The Structural Spine

The official Steam description establishes four recurring pieces:

| Component | Official description | Route relevance |
|---|---|---|
| Daytime city | Explore, attend seminars, work jobs, patrol | Skills, money, Charm |
| Night in Ligo | Travel the Dream Corridor and fight monsters | Combat practice and strength |
| Puzzles | Solve them to boost stats | Additional progression |
| Chosen encounters | Track hidden Pact-holders and fight them | Main story and defeat branches |

The Japanese DLsite page describes a finer four-part day: morning, evening, night, and the dream world. This is compatible with the English day/night summary and is useful if you are planning activities rather than treating each calendar day as one choice.

## Chapter Structure

No publisher page reviewed here publishes a chapter list. The community guide at [think-game](https://think-game.com/archives/53900213.html) describes a prologue, chapters 1 through 5, and a final chapter. That is the best available structural description, but it is third-party.

For route planning, the important point is not a secret chapter count. It is that the game has a long main story followed by a finale with multiple reported outcomes. The old "six chapters" shorthand is less precise than the prologue-plus-five-plus-final structure.

## Victories and Defeat Routes

The Japanese DLsite description explicitly states that losing to another Pact-holder does not end the game. The story moves into a defeat route, and Iroha continues looking for a chance to recover her old life.

This changes how a route should be planned. A loss is not automatically a failed run. It opens alternate content, but it also commits the current story segment to that branch until the narrative returns to the main line. Save before a Chosen fight if you want to inspect the result without locking in a long detour.

The DLsite page also describes training against "sukemon" monsters as a way to learn combat fundamentals. No official source gives an exact level requirement for each boss.

## The Finale

The only documented route split is at the end:

- Win the final battle and choose one option.
- Win and choose the other option.
- Lose the final battle.

A community guide labels these ED01, ED02, and ED03. The developer separately describes the two winning outcomes as memory erasure and memory retention. The developer does not map those descriptions to the community's ED01 and ED02 names.

No official source publishes a Charm threshold, affection score, costume requirement, or dialogue checklist for this split. A route guide that claims otherwise needs a first-party citation.

## A Practical Route Plan

There is no verified optimal build. The following plan only uses confirmed systems:

1. Explore daytime activities and learn which ones raise Charm or provide money.
2. Attend seminars and complete stat-boosting puzzles when they are available.
3. Use the Dream Corridor to practise combat before a major Chosen encounter.
4. Buy equipment with the money earned from work.
5. Keep manual saves before major fights so a defeat route can be explored or avoided.
6. Save again immediately before the final battle.
7. After winning, record both post-victory choices. Reload and lose once if you want to verify the reported third ending.

The recollection room is a post-clear goal. The developer confirmed that it cannot be fully unlocked before clearing, so do not treat missing gallery entries as a route mistake during the first run.

## Route Blockers and What Is Not Documented

The official pages do not list quest flags, required side events, or a complete boss order. They also do not document an easy mode, New Game+, or a hidden route selected by virgin status. Those ideas appear in fan discussion, not in the sources reviewed here.

If progression appears blocked, start with concrete checks:

- Confirm you have explored both daytime and nighttime activities.
- Check whether work income can buy a useful equipment upgrade.
- Look for unfinished puzzles or seminars.
- Revisit the current story objective rather than assuming a hidden stat gate.
- Reload a save if a defeat route has pulled the story away from the route you intended.
