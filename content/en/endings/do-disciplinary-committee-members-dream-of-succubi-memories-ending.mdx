## Is There a "Memories Ending"?

No official page uses the phrase **Do Disciplinary Committee Members Dream of Succubi memories ending**. The name is fan shorthand, and it is easy to see why it spread: the developer's own description of the two winning outcomes is built around memory. One branch erases Iroha's memories of the supernatural episode; the other lets her retain them.

There is also an in-game recollection room for reviewing scenes, but it is not called the "Hall of Memories" on any source reviewed here. Older articles that use that name, describe a bad ending returning you to the pre-fight point, or claim a New Game+ clear-data prompt are adding details that are not supported by the official pages or the community guide used below.

## The Source of the Confusion

Three different things get collapsed into the phrase "memories ending."

| Thing | What it actually is | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Memory-erasure win branch | A post-victory outcome described by the developer | Developer statement |
| Memory-retention win branch | The other post-victory outcome described by the developer | Developer statement |
| Recollection room | An in-game gallery for reviewing scenes | Developer-confirmed feature, not an ending |
| "Hall of Memories" | A fan name found in some articles | Not documented |
| "Memories ending" | A fan label | Not an official route name |

The useful answer is to talk about the two memory-based win branches and the separate loss ending reported by a community guide, not to invent a third "memories" route.

## The Two Memory Branches

Milky Way's August 17, 2025 Ci-en reply describes both winning outcomes.

### Memory erasure

Iroha's experience as a part-succubus is effectively wiped. The developer says the surrounding characters' circumstances do not change dramatically and that the ending is essentially the same as nothing happening before or after the story. It is a quiet, status-quo outcome carried by atmosphere.

### Memory retention

Iroha keeps what happened. The wider cast still does not undergo a sweeping change, but the developer says she is likely to become the antagonist's toy instead of Nashi Okane. That is a character-level consequence, not a new world state or a hidden route.

Neither branch is called true, normal, or canon on the official pages.

## The Third Ending Reported by a Community Guide

The Japanese guide at [think-game](https://think-game.com/archives/53900213.html) lists three ending events. ED01 and ED02 are chosen after winning the final battle; ED03 is reached by losing it. This is third-party documentation, so its labels and trigger notes should be attributed to the guide rather than to Kagura Games.

| Community label | Reported trigger |
|---|---|
| ED01 「現代に生きるサキュバス」 | Win the final battle, then choose |
| ED02 「帰ってきた日常」 | Win the final battle, then choose the other option |
| ED03 「永遠の悪夢」 | Lose the final battle |

The guide does not map ED01 and ED02 to memory erasure or retention. It also does not support the claim that losing returns you to the pre-fight point or records a bad ending in a gallery. Those details should be treated as unverified unless a player reproduces them.

## How the Recollection Room Works

The recollection room is the gallery where viewed scenes can be reviewed. The developer confirmed on September 17, 2026 that it cannot be fully opened before clearing the game. That is the only firm unlock rule established by the sources reviewed here.

No official source describes a New Game+ prompt, a clear-data bonus, or a route selector inside the room. If a guide claims those features, check whether it is describing a mod, a different game, or a guess.

## How to See the Memory Branches

The safe plan is save-based:

1. Keep a manual save before the final battle.
2. Win and choose one option.
3. Reload and choose the other option.
4. Reload and lose if you want to verify the community-reported ED03.
5. Finish the game once before expecting a complete recollection room.

Because the official pages do not publish flag values, no guide can honestly promise an exact stat threshold for either memory branch. The choice after the final battle is the trigger reported by the community guide; the developer's reply explains the aftermath rather than the selection logic.

## FAQ

**Is there a memories ending?**
No ending by that name is documented. The phrase likely refers to the memory-erasure and memory-retention win branches.

**Is the recollection room called the Hall of Memories?**
No. No source reviewed here uses that name. It is the recollection room.

**How many endings does the game have?**
No official count exists. The developer describes two winning branches, and a community guide lists those plus a loss ending.

**Does losing the final boss return me to the pre-fight point?**
That claim is not supported by the sources reviewed here. The community guide only lists a loss ending; it does not document the retry behavior.

**Can I unlock the full gallery before finishing?**
No. The developer confirmed that the recollection room cannot be fully opened before clearing the game.
