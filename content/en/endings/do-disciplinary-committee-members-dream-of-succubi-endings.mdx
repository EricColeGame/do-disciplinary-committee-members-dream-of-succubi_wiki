## What Do Disciplinary Committee Members Dream of Succubi Endings Actually Include?

The official English store page does not publish an ending count, an ending list, or named routes. It asks whether Iroha will survive or suffer a tragic defeat at the hands of the other Chosen, and it says she may eventually have to decide "which side of her is her real self." Those lines establish the themes, but they are not a spoiler guide.

The most specific information comes from two different places. In an August 17, 2025 reply on Milky Way's Ci-en page, the developer describes two winning branches: a memory-erasure ending and a memory-retention ending. A separate Japanese community guide, [think-game's ending and scene list](https://think-game.com/archives/53900213.html), lists three ending events, including one reached by losing the final battle. That guide is third-party documentation, not an official statement.

That is the honest baseline for Do Disciplinary Committee Members Dream of Succubi endings: two developer-described win outcomes, one community-reported loss outcome, and no official label for any of them.

## The Three Ending Events Reported So Far

The community guide lists the ending events with Japanese titles and trigger notes. Its labels are useful because they distinguish the two post-victory choices from the loss branch.

| Community label | Reported trigger | Relationship to the developer's comments |
|---|---|---|
| ED01 「現代に生きるサキュバス」 | Win the final battle, then choose an option | A post-victory branch; the guide does not map it to memory erasure or retention |
| ED02 「帰ってきた日常」 | Win the final battle, then choose the other option | The other post-victory branch; again, the mapping is not stated |
| ED03 「永遠の悪夢」 | Lose the final battle | A loss ending, separate from the two win branches |

The developer's reply confirms that the two win branches are built around memory, but it does not publish English menu names or a one-to-one mapping to the guide's ED01 and ED02 labels. Do not assume that "normal," "true," or "bad" are official terms. They are player shorthand.

The same community guide describes the story structure as a prologue, chapters 1 through 5, and a final chapter. That is more precise than the older "six chapters" phrasing used by some fan summaries, but it is still third-party documentation rather than publisher copy.

## Memory Erasure vs. Memory Retention

The developer's reply is unusually direct about the two winning outcomes. Neither one reshapes the world in a dramatic way. The memory-erasure ending is described as essentially returning the story to its pre-event status quo, with atmosphere carrying the emotional weight. The memory-retention ending keeps Iroha's experiences intact and implies that she becomes the antagonist's toy instead of Nashi Okane.

That difference matters more for character aftermath than for a large branch map. It also explains why community discussions often call the retention route the more open-ended one without declaring it canonical.

### What the official pages do not say

- They do not say there are exactly two endings.
- They do not say there are exactly three endings.
- They do not name a "true ending," "normal ending," or "memories ending."
- They do not publish the flags that select a branch.

The responsible way to describe the game is therefore: two developer-described win branches, plus a third loss branch reported by a community guide.

## Defeat Routes Are Not the Same as Endings

The Japanese DLsite description explains that losing to another Pact-holder does not simply stop the story. It shifts the narrative into a defeat route, where Iroha endures the consequences and continues looking for a way back. Those routes are substantial alternate content, but most of them are not final endings.

The final battle is the exception reported by the community guide: losing there is listed as ED03, not as a mid-story detour. Keep that distinction in mind when reading forum posts that use "defeat route" and "bad ending" interchangeably.

## How to Plan a Run Without Guessing

There is no official flag list, so the practical plan is save-based rather than route-based.

1. Keep a manual save before the final battle.
2. Win once and record which choice produces which ending event.
3. Reload and take the other choice if you want the second win outcome.
4. Reload again and lose if you want to verify the community-reported loss ending.
5. Finish the game once before expecting a complete recollection room; the developer confirmed on September 17, 2026 that it cannot be fully opened before clearing.

The recollection room is the in-game gallery. It is not called the "Hall of Memories" on any source reviewed here, and no official page documents a New Game+ system.

## FAQ

**How many endings does Do Disciplinary Committee Members Dream of Succubi have?**
There is no official count. The developer describes two winning branches, and a Japanese community guide lists those plus a third ending reached by losing the final battle.

**Is there an official true ending?**
No official page labels one branch as true or canonical. The developer discusses both winning outcomes without ranking them.

**Can I unlock every ending in one playthrough?**
The two winning outcomes are alternate choices, and the third is reached by losing, so a single uninterrupted run cannot show all three. Save before the final battle and reload.

**Is there a memories ending?**
No ending by that name is documented. "Memories" most likely refers to the memory-erasure and memory-retention descriptions in the developer's reply, or to the in-game recollection room.

**Do I need to lose battles to finish the game?**
No. Losing opens defeat content, but the main story can be completed by winning. The loss ending is an alternate outcome, not a requirement for every run.
