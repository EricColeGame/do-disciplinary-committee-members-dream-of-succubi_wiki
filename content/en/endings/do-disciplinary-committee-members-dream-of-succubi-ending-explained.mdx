If you have just finished Milky Way's RPG and want a clean explanation, start with the limit: there is no official ending list. The English store copy asks whether Iroha will survive or suffer a tragic defeat, and whether she can decide which side of herself is real. It does not name the endings or say how many exist.

The useful evidence comes from two layers. Milky Way's own Ci-en reply describes two winning outcomes built around memory. A Japanese community guide lists three ending events: two chosen after beating the final battle and one reached by losing it. This guide keeps those layers separate so a player-reported branch is not mistaken for publisher copy.

**Spoiler warning:** this page discusses the final battle and the aftermath described by the developer and a community guide.

## The Short Answer

The game has two developer-described winning branches and a third ending reported by a community guide.

| Layer | What it establishes | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Official store copy | Survival or tragic defeat is possible; Iroha's identity is the thematic question | Steam and Kagura Games |
| Developer reply | Two winning outcomes: memory erasure and memory retention | Milky Way on Ci-en, August 17, 2025 |
| Community guide | Three ending events: ED01, ED02, ED03 | think-game, third-party |

There is no official confirmation of a "true ending," "normal ending," or "memories ending." Those are fan labels. The recollection room is also not called the "Hall of Memories" on any source reviewed here.

## The Two Winning Branches

The developer's reply is the strongest evidence for what happens after a win. It describes the outcomes in terms of memory rather than good versus evil.

### Memory erasure

In the memory-erasure branch, the supernatural episode is effectively removed from Iroha's ordinary life. The developer says the surrounding characters' circumstances do not change dramatically and that the ending is essentially the same as nothing happening before or after the story. The emotional effect comes from atmosphere rather than a transformed world.

### Memory retention

In the memory-retention branch, Iroha keeps what she experienced. The developer again says the wider cast does not undergo a sweeping change, but adds that Iroha is likely to become the antagonist's toy instead of Nashi Okane. That is a character-level consequence, not a new world state.

Neither branch is called canonical on the official store pages. The developer explains both as valid outcomes.

## The Community-Reported Third Ending

The Japanese guide at [think-game](https://think-game.com/archives/53900213.html) lists the ending events with trigger notes. Its page is third-party documentation, so treat the names and triggers as community-reported even when they line up with the developer's comments.

| Community label | Reported trigger | What it adds |
|---|---|---|
| ED01 「現代に生きるサキュバス」 | Beat the final battle, then choose an option | One post-victory ending |
| ED02 「帰ってきた日常」 | Beat the final battle, then choose the other option | The other post-victory ending |
| ED03 「永遠の悪夢」 | Lose the final battle | A loss ending |

The guide does not establish which of ED01 and ED02 corresponds to memory erasure or memory retention. It also does not publish internal flag values. Its value is that it records a third ending event that the official pages do not mention.

## What Determines the Branch?

The official pages do not publish the selection logic. The developer describes the outcomes, not the conditions. The community guide says the two win endings are selected by a choice after the final battle and that the loss ending follows a defeat there.

That gives players a practical, if incomplete, model:

1. Save before the final battle.
2. Win and take one choice to see one post-victory ending.
3. Reload and take the other choice for the second.
4. Reload and lose if you want to verify ED03.

Do not trust a guide that claims a precise stat threshold or hidden item checklist without showing evidence. No official source reviewed for this page provides one.

## Defeat Routes, Virgin Runs, and Other Non-Endings

The Japanese DLsite description explains that losing to a Pact-holder moves the story into a defeat route rather than a game-over screen. Those routes are alternate content, but they are not all final endings. The final-battle loss is the branch the community guide labels ED03.

A virgin playthrough is also not a separate scripted ending. The developer has described it as a self-imposed challenge, and community reports treat it as a harder way to play rather than a fourth finale. A run can be challenging without changing the ending list.

The recollection room is a gallery for reviewing scenes. The developer confirmed on September 17, 2026 that it cannot be fully opened before clearing the game. No official source documents a New Game+ system, so do not assume clear data unlocks one.

## FAQ

**How many endings does Do Disciplinary Committee Members Dream of Succubi have?**
No official count exists. The developer describes two winning branches, and a community guide lists those plus a loss ending.

**Is there a true or golden ending?**
No official page ranks one branch above the others. The developer explains both winning outcomes without naming a canon route.

**Does losing the final battle end the game?**
The community guide lists a loss ending, ED03. That is different from the mid-story defeat routes, which continue the narrative rather than ending it.

**Is a virgin run an ending?**
No. It is a self-imposed challenge, not a separate scripted finale.

**Can I see all endings in one run?**
No. The two winning outcomes are alternate choices and the third is a loss, so use separate saves before the final battle.

The clean explanation is therefore narrower than many fan guides suggest: two developer-described win branches, one community-reported loss branch, and no official canon label. That is enough to plan a run without inventing a fourth ending or a hidden gallery name.
