The **Do Disciplinary Committee Members Dream of Succubi true ending** is a fan phrase, not an official route. Neither Steam nor Kagura Games ranks the finales or calls one canonical. The official copy asks whether Iroha will survive or suffer a tragic defeat and whether she can decide which side of herself is real. That is thematic framing, not an ending list.

The developer's Ci-en reply supplies the clearest concrete information: two winning outcomes, one built around erasing Iroha's memories and one around retaining them. A Japanese community guide adds a third ending event reached by losing the final battle. The correct answer is therefore not "the true ending is X"; it is "there is no official canon label, but there are three documented or reported ending events."

## Is There an Official True Ending?

No. The official pages do not use "true ending," "golden ending," or "canon ending." They also do not publish an ending count. A guide can recommend one branch for its emotional payoff, but that is opinion rather than an official designation.

## The Two Developer-Described Win Branches

Milky Way's August 17, 2025 reply describes the two winning outcomes by their aftermath.

### Memory erasure

Iroha's supernatural experience is effectively removed from her ordinary life. The developer says the surrounding cast's situation does not change dramatically and describes the ending as essentially the same as nothing happening before or after the story. Its weight comes from atmosphere rather than a reshaped world.

### Memory retention

Iroha keeps her memories. The wider cast still does not undergo a sweeping change, but the developer says Iroha is likely to become the antagonist's toy instead of Nashi Okane. That is the more open-ended and more troubling implication, but it is not labeled better or canon.

## The Reported Loss Ending

The Japanese community guide at [think-game](https://think-game.com/archives/53900213.html) records three ending events. Its third entry, ED03 「永遠の悪夢」, is reached by losing the final battle. That is distinct from the mid-story defeat routes described on DLsite, which continue the narrative after a loss rather than ending it.

| Community label | Trigger | Source status |
|---|---|---|
| ED01 「現代に生きるサキュバス」 | Win the final battle and choose | Third-party |
| ED02 「帰ってきた日常」 | Win the final battle and choose the other option | Third-party |
| ED03 「永遠の悪夢」 | Lose the final battle | Third-party |

The guide does not say which of ED01 and ED02 is memory erasure or memory retention. Do not treat its labels as official English names.

## How to See All Three

The practical method is save management, not a secret checklist.

1. Keep a manual save before the final battle.
2. Win and take one choice.
3. Reload and take the other choice.
4. Reload and lose to verify the community-reported loss ending.
5. Finish once before expecting the recollection room to be complete. The developer confirmed on September 17, 2026 that it cannot be fully opened before clearing.

No official source reviewed here documents a New Game+ mode, a "Hall of Memories," or a hidden fourth ending. The recollection room is simply the gallery where scenes can be reviewed.

## Virgin Runs and Defeat Routes

A virgin playthrough is a self-imposed challenge, not a true-ending requirement. Community reports treat it as a harder way to play, and the developer has described it in challenge-run terms rather than as a separate scripted finale.

Defeat routes are also not all endings. The Japanese DLsite description says losing to a Pact-holder moves the story into a defeat route, where Iroha endures the consequences and keeps going. The final-battle loss is the specific branch the community guide labels ED03.
