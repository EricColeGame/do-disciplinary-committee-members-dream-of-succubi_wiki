The phrase **Do Disciplinary Committee Members Dream of Succubi normal ending** is a search problem before it is a story problem. No official page uses "normal ending," and no official page publishes an ending list. Players use the phrase loosely for the straightforward win route, usually the choice that returns Iroha to an ordinary life rather than leaving her tied to the demonic conflict.

The better question is not "which ending is normal?" but "which branch am I looking at?" The developer describes two winning outcomes, and a Japanese community guide records three ending events. This page separates those sources and shows how to plan a run without treating fan shorthand as canon.

## Is There an Official Normal Ending?

No. The [Steam store page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4233960/Do_Disciplinary_Committee_Members_Dream_of_Succubi/) and Kagura Games' product page describe the premise, the Chosen battles, and the identity question. They do not label an ending "normal," "true," or "bad," and they do not give a total.

## What Players Usually Mean by "Normal Ending"

The label is most often applied to one of the two post-victory outcomes: the one in which Iroha's supernatural experiences are erased and ordinary life resumes. That matches the developer's description of the memory-erasure branch, where the surrounding situation is essentially unchanged and the story returns to its prior status quo.

Some players use "normal" for the opposite win branch because it keeps the story open-ended. Others use it for any ending that is not the loss route. Because the term has no fixed definition, a guide that presents a "normal ending" without saying which trigger it means is not precise enough to follow.

### A useful vocabulary

- **Win branch:** one of the two outcomes reached after beating the final battle.
- **Loss branch:** the ending the community guide lists after losing the final battle.
- **Defeat route:** mid-story alternate content after losing to a Chosen; usually not a final ending.
- **Recollection room:** the gallery for reviewing scenes, not a route selector.

## The Developer's Two Winning Outcomes

Milky Way's August 17, 2025 Ci-en reply describes both winning outcomes. In the memory-erasure version, the episode is effectively erased and little changes around Iroha. In the memory-retention version, she keeps her experiences and is implied to become the antagonist's toy instead of Nashi Okane.

The developer does not call either one normal or true. Both are presented as consequences of the same larger story, with the difference concentrated in what Iroha remembers and what that implies for her future.

## The Community-Reported Loss Ending

The Japanese community guide at [think-game](https://think-game.com/archives/53900213.html) lists three ending events. ED01 and ED02 are selected after winning the final battle; ED03 is reached by losing it. The guide's English titles are not official localizations, and its mapping to the memory branches is not stated.

That third event is why older articles claiming there are only two endings are incomplete. The developer confirms two win branches, but the community guide records a loss branch as well.

## Planning a "Normal" Run

If "normal" means the quieter, status-quo win outcome, the practical route is straightforward:

1. Build enough combat strength to win the final battle. The official loop gives you money from daytime work, skills from daytime activities, stats from puzzles, and combat practice in the Dream Corridor.
2. Keep a manual save before the final battle.
3. Win and choose the option that produces the quieter outcome. If you are unsure which is which, save before the choice and test both.
4. Reload if you want the other win branch or the loss ending.
5. Clear the game once before expecting a complete recollection room; the developer confirmed on September 17, 2026 that it cannot be fully unlocked earlier.

Do not rely on a "six chapters" checklist. The community guide describes a prologue, chapters 1 through 5, and a final chapter. That structure is third-party, but it is more precise than the old six-chapter shorthand.

## What the Game Does Not Document

There is no official difficulty menu, no official ending flag list, and no official New Game+ description. A virgin playthrough is a self-imposed challenge, not a hidden normal ending. The recollection room is not a "Hall of Memories," and no source reviewed here uses that name.

The Japanese DLsite description does confirm that losing to a Chosen shifts the story into a defeat route instead of a game over. That is useful context, but it should not be confused with the final loss ending.

## FAQ

**Is there an official normal ending?**
No. "Normal" is player shorthand. The official pages do not name any ending.

**Which ending is the normal one?**
Most players mean the quieter post-victory outcome where Iroha's supernatural experience is erased. That matches the developer's memory-erasure description, but the label is not official.

**How many endings are there?**
No official count exists. The developer describes two winning branches, and a community guide lists those plus a loss ending.

**Can I see every ending in one playthrough?**
No. The two win outcomes are alternate choices and the third is a loss, so save before the final battle.

**Is there a virgin ending?**
No. A virgin run is a self-imposed challenge, not a scripted ending.
