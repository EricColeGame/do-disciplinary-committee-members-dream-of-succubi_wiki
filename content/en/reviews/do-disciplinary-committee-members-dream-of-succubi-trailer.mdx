## What the Trailer Is Advertising

The trailer accompanies a store description about Iroha Himeno, a disciplinary committee member who meets Lily the succubus and accepts a Pact. The Pact makes her part succubus and requires her to fight the Chosen, other humans who made pacts with demons.

The written page also describes the day/night loop: city activities by day, Ligo and the Dream Corridor by night, puzzles for stat boosts, work for equipment money, and Charm growth. Those details are the safest summary of the game the trailer is promoting.

## Trailer vs. Gameplay

A trailer is a short marketing edit. It is not a full demonstration of the day/night schedule, the defeat routes, or the ending structure. Do not infer a complete feature list from pacing or scene selection.

For gameplay, rely on the written store description and the Japanese DLsite page. DLsite identifies RPG Maker MV 1.6.1, roughly 25 hours of play, 130 adult scenes, 42 standing-art scenes, more than 1,400 variations, and 13 costumes plus extras.

For story, rely on the official premise and clearly label community findings. The developer describes two winning outcomes, and a Japanese community guide reports a third ending reached by losing the final battle.
