## Steam: The Confirmed English Release

Steam lists the game for Windows at S$16.25 at the time reviewed. The page shows English, Japanese, and Simplified Chinese support, plus single-player, Steam Cloud, and Family Sharing. It lists October 8, 2026 as the release date.

The store page describes a daytime city phase and a nighttime phase in Ligo's Dream Corridor. It also names the Chosen, the battle-royale premise, and Charm progression. Those are the verified features to expect from the download.

The game is adults-only. Do not assume that buying it grants permission to redistribute the files, and do not treat a family-sharing feature as a licence for public uploads.

## The Kagura Patch Is Not a Free Game
