## The Short Answer

The English release of Do Disciplinary Committee Members Dream of Succubi launched on **October 8, 2026** according to Steam. Kagura Games' product page uses **October 9, 2026**. That one-day difference is a storefront discrepancy, not evidence of two separate English launches.

The original Japanese PC release arrived earlier, on **May 4, 2025**, as DLsite work RJ01241743. The English version is a later localisation of that game.

## Why the Dates Differ

Storefront dates can reflect different time zones, regional publishing schedules, or the moment a page was updated. The official Steam page is the most precise source for the Steam product, while Kagura's page is the publisher's own listing.

Neither page describes two English editions. The correct conclusion is that the English release window is October 8 to 9, 2026 depending on the storefront, not that one version is a separate game.
