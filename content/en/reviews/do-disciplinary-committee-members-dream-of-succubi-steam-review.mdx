## What the Steam Page Actually Shows

The [Steam store page for Do Disciplinary Committee Members Dream of Succubi](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4233960/Do_Disciplinary_Committee_Members_Dream_of_Succubi/) lists Milky Way as developer, Kagura Games as publisher, and October 8, 2026 as the release date. It is a Windows-only title with English, Japanese, and Simplified Chinese support, plus single-player, Steam Cloud, and Family Sharing.

At the time reviewed, the page showed 160 user recommendations and a price of S$16.25. A reliable positive-review percentage was not available in the data used for this article, so no "mostly positive" or "99% positive" figure is reported.

## The Premise and Loop

Iroha, a disciplinary committee member, meets Lily the succubus and accepts a Pact. Winning the demons' proxy war will grant her wish, but the bargain makes her part succubus and puts her against the Chosen.

The gameplay splits into day and night. Daytime covers city exploration, seminars, jobs, and patrols. Nighttime covers Ligo and the Dream Corridor, where Iroha fights monsters and trains. Money from work buys equipment, daytime experience helps her learn skills, and puzzles provide extra stat boosts.

That is a coherent loop on paper: activities feed combat, combat advances the story, and the story raises the cost of Iroha's transformation.

## What Makes It Distinctive

The most notable mechanical choice is the defeat route. The Japanese DLsite page says losing to a Pact-holder does not end the game. The narrative continues with Iroha humiliated but still looking for a way back to her former life.

This makes a loss feel like a branch rather than a dead end. It also means the player can explore defeat content without save-scumming every fight. At the final battle, however, losing is reported as a separate ending event, so a save before the finale is still wise.

## Content and Length

DLsite lists roughly 25 hours of play, depending on how many optional scenes are collected. It also reports 130 adult scenes with 86 base CG images, 42 standing-art scenes with 7 base images, more than 1,400 variations, and 13 costumes plus extras.

Those numbers suggest a large game for its price, but they do not prove that every scene is essential or that the pacing will suit every player. The game is adults-only, and the mature content is part of the progression.

## Endings and Replay Value

There is no official ending count or true-ending label. The developer describes two winning outcomes: memory erasure and memory retention. A community guide reports a third ending reached by losing the final battle.

The recollection room is the in-game gallery. The developer says it cannot be fully unlocked before clearing. That gives a clear reason to return after the credits without requiring a documented New Game+ mode.

## Platform Limitations

The confirmed English release is Windows on Steam. DLsite sells the original Japanese PC game and a separate Japanese iOS/Android edition. No official English APK, Google Play release, console port, or free full download was found.

Kagura hosts a free v1.05 patch page labelled for Steam and GOG. A GOG release itself was not verified, so Steam remains the confirmed English storefront.

## System Requirements

Steam lists Windows 8/8.1/10/11, an Intel Core2 Duo or better, 4 GB RAM, a DirectX 9 or OpenGL 4.1 capable GPU, and 1 GB of available storage at minimum. The page warns that menu or map loading may cause lag and suggests closing other programs.

Those requirements are modest, which fits the RPG Maker MV 1.6.1 engine identified on DLsite.
