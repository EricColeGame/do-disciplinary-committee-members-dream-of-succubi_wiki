## The Official Trailer Is on Steam

The [Steam page for Do Disciplinary Committee Members Dream of Succubi](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4233960/Do_Disciplinary_Committee_Members_Dream_of_Succubi/) hosts two official trailers: one labelled EN and one labelled CN. They are the primary video sources reviewed here.

The EN trailer is the English-facing preview. The CN trailer is a separate Chinese-labelled version. Their presence confirms that the publisher released official video material, but it does not establish a full scene-by-scene script or a track list.

## What the Store Page Confirms

The trailer sits alongside a detailed store description. That description is the reliable way to understand the game's premise:

- Iroha is a disciplinary committee member.
- She meets Lily, a succubus, on patrol.
- Lily forms a Pact that makes Iroha part succubus.
- Iroha must defeat the Chosen, humans who made pacts with demons.
- Daytime activities build skills and money; nighttime training happens in Ligo.

The trailer may illustrate those ideas, but this article does not invent frame-level details that the official page does not document. If you want to verify a visual, watch the embedded video rather than trusting a search snippet.

## What the Trailer Does Not Prove

A trailer is marketing material. It can be edited, shortened, or built from selected scenes. It does not prove:

- The total number of endings.
- A named true ending.
- The existence of an easy mode.
- A playable character not mentioned on the store page.
- A separate soundtrack release.
- An English mobile or console version.

Those claims need their own sources. The trailer is evidence of official audiovisual material, not a complete product specification.

## Release and Platform Facts

The English release is Steam app 4233960 for Windows. Steam lists October 8, 2026; Kagura's product page uses October 9. The game supports English, Japanese, and Simplified Chinese and includes single-player, Steam Cloud, and Family Sharing.
