## The Cast List the Publisher Actually Provides

The English [Kagura Games product page](https://www.kaguragames.com/product/do-disciplinary-committee-members-dream-of-succubi-patch/) names four characters individually: Iroha Himeno, Lily, Yuno Aiba, and Nashi Okane. The same page describes the Chosen as a group rather than publishing a full named roster.

That is the reliable core of the Do Disciplinary Committee Members Dream of Succubi cast. Fan pages often add shopkeepers, unnamed Chosen, or characters from a different game; those names should not be treated as official without a source.

## Iroha Himeno

Iroha is the center of the story. Kagura describes her as a straight-laced disciplinary committee member who values morals and patrols for people disturbing public order. While on patrol, she meets Lily and accepts a Pact that makes her part succubus.

Her goal is not simply to become stronger. The Pact promises that any wish of hers will be granted if she wins the demons' proxy war. The official store asks whether she will survive, suffer a tragic defeat, or eventually decide which side of herself is real.

Mechanically, Iroha is the character through whom every system is expressed. She attends seminars, works jobs, patrols, solves puzzles, trains in the Dream Corridor, and fights the Chosen. Charm reflects her growing demonic side. The cast page does not need a secret stat build to explain her role: she is the player's view of the entire loop.

## Lily

Lily is the succubus who forges the Pact. Kagura says she scouts humans to fight in the demons' proxy wars and does not exactly support Iroha with everything she has, believing that suffering builds character.

That profile is important because it prevents two common misreadings. Lily is not a conventional helpful mascot, and she is not merely a villain who tricked Iroha. She is the recruiter and the source of the game's central bargain, with a deliberately ambiguous approach to Iroha's growth.

The official spelling is **Lily**. The search term "Lilly" is common, but it is a variant spelling rather than a second character.

## Yuno Aiba

Yuno is described as Iroha's best friend, though the two have not spent much time together lately. After inheriting her parents' shrine, she has to deal with crippling debt and other difficult duties.

Her profile gives the story a grounded concern outside the demonic battle royale. The official page does not say that Yuno is playable, that she has a route ending, or that a particular choice determines her fate. Those claims should not be attached to her without evidence.

## Nashi Okane

Nashi is a brilliant girl of low standing who has become the victim of relentless bullying by the wealthy. Kagura's profile asks whether Iroha will do nothing to save her.

That question makes Nashi a moral test for the protagonist, not just background decoration. The developer's August 17, 2025 Ci-en reply also mentions Nashi when describing the memory-retention outcome, saying Iroha is likely to become the antagonist's toy instead of Nashi. That is a developer-stated consequence, but it is not a full route guide.

## The Chosen

The Chosen are humans who made pacts with demons, just as Iroha did. The official page says their identities are hidden and that Iroha must track them down. Their reveals may shock her.

The store does not publish a complete list of names, pact powers, or boss order. That absence is why a cast article should stop at the confirmed group role. Treating every antagonist mentioned in a forum thread as an official cast member creates the same contamination problem as inventing a character from a different game.

## How the Cast Supports the Loop

The four named characters map onto different parts of the experience:

- Iroha carries the day/night progression and the identity question.
- Lily explains the Pact and the proxy war.
- Yuno grounds Iroha in an ordinary life with ordinary obligations.
- Nashi raises the moral stakes of Iroha's choices.
- The Chosen provide the battle-royale structure and hidden-identity reveals.

This is a small named cast by RPG standards, but it is enough to support a roughly 25-hour game because most scenes focus on Iroha's changing relationship with the world around her.

## Versions and Availability

The English release is Steam app 4233960 for Windows, published by Kagura Games. It supports English, Japanese, and Simplified Chinese and includes single-player, Steam Cloud, and Family Sharing.

The original Japanese PC version is DLsite RJ01241743. DLsite also lists a separate Japanese iOS/Android edition, RJ01398608. That mobile SKU is not an English release, and no official English APK or console version was found.
