## Lilly or Lily? Start With the Official Spelling

The character's official English name is **Lily**. Kagura Games uses that spelling on its product page, and the [Steam description](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4233960/Do_Disciplinary_Committee_Members_Dream_of_Succubi/) does the same. "Lilly" is a common search variant, but no official source reviewed here treats it as a separate character.

That distinction is small but useful. A search for Do Disciplinary Committee Members Dream of Succubi Lilly may surface the right game, yet a fan page can compound the error by inventing a second succubus or assigning the wrong profile to the doubled-consonant name.

## Lily's Official Role

Kagura describes Lily as a succubus who scouts humans to fight in the demons' proxy wars. She meets Iroha during a disciplinary patrol and forges a Pact with her: if Iroha wins the proxy war as an agent for the succubi, any wish of hers will be granted.

Lily does not exactly support Iroha with all she has. The official profile says she believes suffering builds character. That line is the key to her personality. She is a recruiter and a source of harsh encouragement, not a soft mentor who solves the protagonist's problems.

## What the Pact Means

The Pact gives the story its shape:

- Iroha gains demonic abilities and becomes part succubus.
- She must defeat other humans who made pacts with demons.
- Those rivals are called the Chosen.
- The reward is the fulfilment of any wish Iroha chooses.

The official page does not publish the exact wording of the Pact, its termination clause, or a hidden condition based on Lily's affection. It presents the bargain at a high level and lets the story explore the consequences.

## Lily and Iroha's Character Arc

Iroha begins as a rule-focused disciplinary committee member. Lily's Pact forces her into a role that violates the simple categories she used before. The official ending question, "which side of her is her real self," is therefore tied to Lily's intervention even though Lily is not the final antagonist.

The developer's Ci-en reply about the winning branches focuses on Iroha's memory, not on a Lily romance route. No official source describes Lily as having a separate ending route or a hidden playable campaign.

## Lily in the Day/Night Loop

The official loop places Iroha in the city by day and in the succubus town of Ligo at night. The Japanese DLsite description says the nighttime training dungeon was prepared by the succubi, and it describes Lily pushing Iroha to become more active as a succubus.

That makes Lily part of the game's progression logic as well as its plot. She represents the demand to train, use demonic power, and become stronger. The exact scenes in which she appears are not enumerated on the English store page, so this article does not invent a scene list.

## What Lily Is Not

There is no verified official evidence that:

- Lily is the final boss.
- Lily has a separate route ending.
- Lilly is a second character.
- Lily is playable in a post-game campaign.
- A particular gift or dialogue choice unlocks a Lily ending.

Those ideas may appear in fan speculation, but they are not supported by the official pages reviewed here. The recollection room is a gallery for scenes, not evidence of a Lily route.
