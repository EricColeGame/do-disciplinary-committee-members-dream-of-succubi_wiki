## What Is Known About the Characters

The official English character information for Do Disciplinary Committee Members Dream of Succubi is compact. Kagura Games profiles Iroha Himeno, Lily, Yuno Aiba, and Nashi Okane, while the [Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4233960/Do_Disciplinary_Committee_Members_Dream_of_Succubi/) explains the Pact and the Chosen.

There is no official encyclopedia page listing every minor NPC. That makes source discipline important. A name found in a forum thread, an unrelated succubus game, or an image caption is not automatically part of this cast.

## Iroha Himeno: The Disciplinary Committee Member

Iroha is a student who values order and patrols for people disturbing public morals. Her encounter with Lily gives her demonic abilities and places her in a proxy war against other Pact-holders.

Her arc is framed by a question: after becoming more demonic, will she decide which side of herself is real? The game expresses that question through the day/night loop, Charm growth, and the ending branches rather than through a simple morality meter.

The official page does not say that Iroha is a silent protagonist, that the player chooses her name, or that she has a fixed romantic route. Those are assumptions imported from other RPGs.

## Lily: The Succubus Recruiter

Lily comes from the demon realm and recruits humans to fight in the demons' proxy wars. Her official profile says she does not fully support Iroha because she believes suffering builds character.

This makes her a useful source of exposition and a morally ambiguous guide. She is not described as a conventional party member who follows every order. The game's central Pact is her arrangement, and the story's consequences flow from it.

The official spelling is Lily. Search results using "Lilly" usually mean the same character, but the doubled consonant is not the publisher's spelling.

## Yuno Aiba: The Friend With a Shrine

Yuno is technically Iroha's best friend, but the two have not hung out much recently. She inherited her parents' shrine and now deals with crippling debt and other duties.

Her profile introduces ordinary responsibilities that contrast with Iroha's supernatural problem. The official page does not describe Yuno as a combat party member, a secret Chosen, or an ending route. Any of those claims needs separate evidence.

## Nashi Okane: The Bullying Victim

Nashi is brilliant but of low standing, and wealthy students bully her relentlessly. The official profile asks whether Iroha will do nothing to save her.

That question gives Nashi a thematic role: she tests whether Iroha's commitment to public morals includes protecting someone who has no power. The developer later references Nashi when discussing the memory-retention ending, which confirms that her situation is connected to the story's consequences. It does not confirm a separate playable route.

## The Chosen

The Chosen are humans who made pacts with demons. Iroha must find them because their identities are hidden. The official description says the reveals may shock her.

They are the source of the battle-royale structure. The store does not name every Chosen, describe their pact powers, or publish a boss order. A characters article can therefore explain their role without inventing a roster.

## FAQ

**Who are the main characters?**
Iroha Himeno is the protagonist. Lily is the succubus who makes the Pact. Yuno Aiba and Nashi Okane are the other individually profiled characters.

**Are the Chosen playable?**
No. They are the hidden rival Pact-holders Iroha fights.

**Is Lilly a different character from Lily?**
No official source presents them as different. Kagura uses Lily.

**Is there an official full character list?**
No complete encyclopedia roster was found. The official pages individually profile four characters and describe the Chosen as a group.
