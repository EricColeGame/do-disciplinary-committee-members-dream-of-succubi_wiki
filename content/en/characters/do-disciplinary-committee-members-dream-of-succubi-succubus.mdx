## The Succubus Premise in One Paragraph

Do Disciplinary Committee Members Dream of Succubi begins when Iroha, a disciplinary committee member, meets a succubus named Lily. Lily forms a Pact with her: win the demons' proxy war as an agent for the succubi, and Iroha's wish will be granted. The bargain makes Iroha part succubus and puts her in conflict with the Chosen, other humans who made pacts with demons.

That is the official [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4233960/Do_Disciplinary_Committee_Members_Dream_of_Succubi/) and Kagura Games premise. It is also the limit of what the storefronts explain about succubus society. The game does not publish a lore encyclopedia with every faction, noble house, or demon rank.

## Lily Is the Named Succubus

Kagura profiles Lily as a succubus who scouts humans to fight in the demons' proxy wars. She does not fully support Iroha, because she believes suffering builds character.

This makes her role distinct from a generic monster. She is the recruiter, the source of the Pact, and a deliberately harsh influence on the protagonist's growth. The official spelling is Lily; "Lilly" is a search variant, not a second named succubus.

No official source reviewed here names a second major succubus character, a succubus queen, or a playable succubus faction. The Chosen are humans, not a separate succubus roster.

## Ligo and the Dream Corridor

At night, Iroha travels to the succubus town of Ligo. The Dream Corridor there is the official training area where she fights monsters and improves her combat skills.

The Japanese DLsite page adds that the succubi prepared a training dungeon and monsters called "sukemon" to teach the fundamentals of battle. It also describes entering residents' nightmares to confront the source of those dreams.

These details establish the succubi as organisers of Iroha's training, not merely enemies. The game's supernatural world includes a town, a training space, and dream-related missions, but the official pages do not provide a full map or political hierarchy.

## Why Iroha Becomes Part Succubus

The Pact is the reason. Iroha is not born a succubus and does not simply inherit the power. Lily's bargain gives her demonic abilities so she can fight the Chosen.

The story frames that transformation as a question of identity. Iroha starts with a strict sense of public morals, then becomes more demonic as she grows stronger. The official page says she may eventually have to decide which side of her is her real self.

Charm is the visible stat connected to that progression. Work, training, and other activities raise it. No official source publishes a required Charm threshold for a particular ending.

## Defeat and the Succubus War

The official battle-royale framing says fights against the Chosen are to the death. Yet losing to a Pact-holder does not produce a standard game over. The Japanese store explains that the story enters a defeat route, where Iroha is humiliated but continues looking for a way back to her old life.

That combination is unusual: the rhetoric is lethal, while the mechanical consequence is a story branch. It keeps the succubus war dangerous without forcing the player to reload after every loss.

The final battle is the exception reported by a community guide. Losing there is listed as ED03, while winning leads to a choice between two other ending events.

## Adult Content and the Succubus Theme

The game is adults-only. DLsite lists 130 adult scenes with 86 base CG images, plus 42 standing-art scenes with 7 base images, more than 1,400 variations, and 13 costumes plus extras.

Those figures explain the work's scale, but they are not a substitute for story analysis. The succubus premise is tied to Iroha's transformation and the Pact, not to a documented faction route or a separate succubus campaign.
