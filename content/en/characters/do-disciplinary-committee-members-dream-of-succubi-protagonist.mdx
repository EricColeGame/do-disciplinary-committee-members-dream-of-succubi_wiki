## Iroha Himeno Is the Protagonist

The protagonist of Do Disciplinary Committee Members Dream of Succubi is **Iroha Himeno**, a disciplinary committee member at her school. Kagura Games describes her as a straight-laced girl who values public morals and patrols for people causing trouble.

Her ordinary routine changes when she meets Lily, a succubus from the demon realm. Lily forges a Pact with her: Iroha will fight in the demons' proxy war as an agent for the succubi, and in exchange her wish will be granted. The Pact makes her part succubus and turns the other Pact-holders, the Chosen, into her targets.

That is the complete official starting premise. The [Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4233960/Do_Disciplinary_Committee_Members_Dream_of_Succubi/) does not give her age, a fixed birthday, a family history, or a canon romantic partner.

## Why She Fits the Game's Structure

Iroha is not a blank avatar in a visual novel. The official description ties her personality to the central conflict. She values order, yet the Pact requires her to operate outside ordinary rules. She wants to restore her former life, but becoming stronger also makes her more demonic.

The store states the identity question directly: by the end, she may have to decide which side of her is her real self. That is the thematic spine of the character, and it explains why Charm and adult content are part of progression rather than detached extras.

## Her Role in the Day/Night Loop

Iroha is the character through whom every system is expressed:

| Period | What Iroha does | What it develops |
|---|---|---|
| Daytime | Patrols, works jobs, attends seminars | Money, skills, Charm, public order |
| Night | Visits Ligo and the Dream Corridor | Combat strength and training |
| Puzzles | Solves stat-boosting challenges | Additional growth |
| Story | Hunts hidden Chosen | Main plot and defeat branches |

The Japanese DLsite page adds that the day is divided into morning, evening, night, and dream time. Iroha is the constant across those blocks, which is why the game feels like a character-driven schedule rather than a series of disconnected dungeons.

## What Happens When She Loses

Losing to a Pact-holder does not end the game. The Japanese store says the story enters a defeat route, with Iroha humiliated and searching for a way back to her former life. That keeps the protagonist active after failure instead of replacing the scene with a game-over screen.

At the final battle, the loss is reported by a community guide as ED03. The two winning outcomes are selected by a post-victory choice. The developer describes those outcomes as memory erasure and memory retention, both centered on what Iroha remembers.

## Charm and the Identity Theme

The official page says work, training, and other activities raise Charm and make Iroha more demonic. It does not publish a Charm cap or a specific value required for an ending.

That means the honest character analysis is thematic, not numerical. Charm represents the direction of her transformation, but no source says "Charm 50 unlocks the true ending" or "avoid all adult scenes for the normal ending." Those are fan inventions unless backed by a first-party statement.

## Relationships

The official profiles establish four key relationships:

- **Lily:** the succubus who recruits Iroha and believes suffering builds character.
- **Yuno Aiba:** Iroha's best friend, now burdened by a shrine and debt.
- **Nashi Okane:** a brilliant student targeted by wealthy bullies, whose fate tests Iroha's morals.
- **The Chosen:** hidden Pact-holders and rivals in the proxy war.

The pages do not define a canonical romance route for Iroha. Adult scenes exist, but the ending documentation is about memory and survival rather than a named partner ending.
